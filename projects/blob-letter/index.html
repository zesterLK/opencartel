Move to bend. Scroll to invert.

Blob Letter

Move across the letter to push the ink. Drag on touch screens. Scroll down to spread the ink across the screen and invert the colours. Scroll back to shrink the ink before it covers the screen. Use Invert or press Enter for the same transition without scrolling. Arrow keys push the letter; Reset restores its shape.

Motion respects your system preference. This demo runs locally in your browser.
