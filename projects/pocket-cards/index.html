Pocket Cards

Download sourceSource

Select a card.

A place for your favourites.

Pick a card to lift it out of the pocket. Pick it again or choose Tuck away to return it. Each card can hold its own detail.

Keyboard: Tab between cards, Enter or Space to select, arrow keys to move, Escape to tuck away. The cards also work with touch. Reduced motion keeps the same states without animated travel.
