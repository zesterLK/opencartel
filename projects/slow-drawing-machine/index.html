Leave a little to chance.

Two linked arms guide one pen. The screen is the canvas. Let a line build, then change the rhythm and see where it goes.

Orbit, Bloom and Weave begin fresh drawings. Tune changes the rhythm or ink and starts a separate stroke, so it never connects distant points with an accidental line. Drag the pen to place it anywhere; it lifts without marking the canvas, then continues from the new position. Speed starts at 0.25× and goes up to 10×. Clear starts again.

Save SVG keeps the exact drawn lines as editable vectors, without the mechanism. Nothing is uploaded. Drawings live only in this tab until you save them.

This is a harmonograph-inspired path with a kinematic mechanism, not a physical pendulum simulation. Reduced motion starts paused. Drawings stop at the point limit to keep the page responsive.
