One last task.

Press the amber button on the shredder. The page feeds through the head, becomes eighteen ribbons, then settles into the bin.

Again? Queue as many sheets as you like with + Sheet or + 5. Pick a speed and add files while the machine runs. Empty bin clears the contents after the current sheet finishes.

Explore · Use Look around or the small + markers. Read a note, open the drawer, wake the monitor, or turn the lamp off for moonlight. Shredding returns the camera to the machine.

Keyboard · Tab to the machine button, then use Enter or Space. All controls work with touch too.

Reduced motion · The machine uses two still steps: halfway through, then settled. Press its button for each step. No camera movement or vibration.

A small illustrated simulation. Example pages are fictional. Selected files stay in this browser; no file data is sent anywhere and originals are never modified or deleted. There are no accounts or saved documents. Older shreds compress into a fixed-size visual pile so you can keep going. Needs a WebGL 2 browser. Paper stays only in this tab.
