# Catalog assets

- `public/previews/words-have-weight.webm` and `words-have-weight.png`: original silent recording and poster of the included Words Have Weight application, captured on 19 October 2026. The nine-second loop shows the real heavy-mode pointer lift and release. Only the stage was recorded; application controls were hidden for capture. It is a preview of the actual project, not a generated concept. Original application code is MIT licensed. The lettering uses Archivo Black; the font and its SIL Open Font License are included in the project's `public/fonts/` directory. See that project's attribution for the upstream font source.
- `public/previews/paper-shredder.webm` and `paper-shredder.webp`: original silent runtime recording and ready-state poster of the included Paper Shredder application, captured on 20 October 2026. The approximately 10.5-second WebM shows the actual page feeding, ribbons releasing, and paper settling in the bin. Only the rendered scene is recorded, at 960 × 409; the poster is a scene-only browser capture. These are original MIT-licensed runtime captures, not generated concepts. See the project's `ATTRIBUTION.md` for the scene and runtime asset provenance.

- `public/previews/velvet.webm`: original silent eight-second recording of the included Velvet renderer, captured on 22 October 2026. The light sweeps over its authored directional stroke and returns to the starting angle. Recorded through the renderer’s existing light-control API with no application overlays; encoded at 960 × 600, 25 fps. This is an original MIT-licensed runtime capture, not a generated concept.
- `public/previews/velvet.jpg`: original browser screenshot of the included Velvet application, captured on 22 October 2026, retained as the video’s poster fallback. It shows a real directional stroke on the procedural burgundy surface and the application controls. See the project’s `ATTRIBUTION.md` for runtime and font notices.

- `public/previews/small-worlds.webm`: original silent 16-second recording of the Small Worlds Canvas renderer, captured on 22 October 2026 at 960 × 600 and encoded at 60 fps after its image-sampling optimization. It travels inward through the postcard and window, then reverses to its opening frame. Captured directly from the actual renderer without interface overlays; the PNG source paintings and editable transforms remain in the project.
- `public/previews/small-worlds.webp`: original scene-only browser capture of the included Small Worlds renderer, captured on 22 October 2026 at 1280 × 800. It composites the production studio and city paintings through their actual recursive portals. See the project’s `ATTRIBUTION.md` for AI-assisted artwork provenance and reuse terms.

No third-party reference screenshots, concept images or research materials are bundled in the site.

## Typography

The catalog bundles the following unmodified font files from the official Google Fonts repository. Each retains its SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `public/fonts/`:

- **Syne** — Bonjour Monde and Lucas Descroix; `Syne-Variable.ttf`, used for the large site title. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/syne . License: `Syne-OFL.txt`.
- **Chakra Petch** — Cadson Demak; `ChakraPetch-Regular.ttf`, used for headings and controls. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/chakrapetch . License: `ChakraPetch-OFL.txt`.
- **Newsreader** — Production Type; `Newsreader-Variable.ttf` and `Newsreader-Italic-Variable.ttf`, used for text and italic connecting words. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader . License: `Newsreader-OFL.txt`.

Variable font filenames are simplified for the catalog; font contents are unchanged. No proprietary reference-site fonts are bundled.

## Interaction inspiration

The interactive title takes inspiration from [Jordan Egstad's portfolio](https://egstad.com/): individual letters select staggered typography effects and a page-color cycle. OpenCartel uses its own implementation, typography, palette and accessible controls. No source code or visual assets from that site are bundled.
