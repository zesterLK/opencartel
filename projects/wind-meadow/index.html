Effects

A little wind.

Drag through the grass or send a gust. Every blade bends from its root and rises again.

Effects lets you change the wind, its pace and the sunlight. Adaptive detail balances the scene with your browser. Light uses fewer blades; Rich asks more of your graphics hardware.

Pause stops the wind. Reduced motion starts still; choose Play when you want motion. All effects are local, procedural and approximate nature.
