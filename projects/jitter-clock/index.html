Drawing the first line…

Count or local time.

A line that won’t sit still.

Each new digit starts quietly, then curls into loose coils. Count follows a one-digit rhythm. Time shows your local 24-hour clock. Pause holds the drawing; speed changes how quickly the count advances.

Keyboard: Space pauses, R resets. Reduced motion keeps the outlines calm. Nothing leaves your device.
