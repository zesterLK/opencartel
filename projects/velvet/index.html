A different kind of mark.

Drag a finger or pointer over the velvet. Each stroke lays its short fibres in a new direction. Brush back the other way and watch the sheen reverse.

Light
Turn the light around the surface to reveal your marks.

Keyboard
Tab to the velvet. Arrow keys move the brush; Space toggles brushing. Hold Shift to move further. Escape stops brushing.

Your strokes stay in this tab until you smooth or reload. This is a stylized material experiment, not a physical cloth simulation. It has no camera movement or automatic animation.
