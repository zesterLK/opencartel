## Artwork

`public/art/studio.png` , `public/art/studio-portrait.png` and `public/art/city.png`: original AI-assisted production paintings created for OpenCartel using OpenAI image generation, 22 October 2026. The studio is adapted from the project’s original generated concept; the portrait composition is an adaptation of that studio for narrow screens, and the city is a companion painting. No third-party reference painting is bundled. The paintings are included for reuse with this project under the repository’s MIT terms; no exclusivity or trademark rights are asserted. Portal cropping and compositing happen at runtime.
